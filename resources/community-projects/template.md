## Goal

_What do you want to achieve, and why does it matter for Flatland or for railway operations?
State the goal so that one can tell at the end whether it has been reached, e.g.
"Provide a baseline policy that solves all ECML 2026 level 1 scenarios without collisions"._

## Description

_Describe the project in a few paragraphs:_

- _**Background / motivation:** what problem or question are you addressing?_
- _**Approach:** how do you plan to tackle it (methods, tools, data, Flatland components involved)?_
- _**Deliverables:** what will be produced (code, dataset, scenarios, paper, documentation, ...)?_
- _**Scope and first steps:** what is in and out of scope, and what are the first concrete steps?_
- _**Skills needed:** what kind of help are you looking for?_
